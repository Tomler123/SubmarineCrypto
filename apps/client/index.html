HOW PAYOUTS WORK

payout = stake × ( 1 + lev × Δ% × dir − oxygen )
Δ% = index change since your entry
dir = +1 Surface · −1 Dive
oxygen = 0.25% per second you stay down

Oxygen is the house edge, and it is the only one. There is no spread on entry, no fee on cash-out, and no haircut on your win. Instead your multiplier drains by 0.25% of your stake every second the position is open — 1.5% after a minute. Time is the cost of playing. Hold for 60s at 10× and the index has to move 1.5% your way just to break even.

Your hull fails (full stake lost) when the index reaches your crush line, drawn on the chart from the moment you enter. It starts 1 / leverage away from your entry — at 10× from an entry of 1000, that is 900 on a Surface. But oxygen makes it creep toward your entry price: after 30s it is 907.5, after 60s 915. The longer you hold, the smaller the move that kills you. Your loss is always capped at your stake.

Cash-out is not instant: the pod takes 0.5s to blow ballast and ascend, and settles at the index when the ascent completes. You can be crushed mid-escape. This delay is the same for everyone — it removes any advantage from faster market feeds.

Phase 1 uses a simulated index and play-money balance. The 0.25%/s oxygen rate is a provisional value and is published here whenever it changes.
